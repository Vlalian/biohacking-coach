Title: Month-aware Coach narrative
Status: future

## What to build

Update the Coach narrative panel in the Training Plan view so the text reflects the specific month being displayed — not just the Training Phase. For example, viewing week 3 of Base Building should read differently from viewing week 1 or the final week before transitioning to Build.

## Why it was deferred

The meaningful version requires knowing the athlete's actual training timeline: phase start date, race date, and which week of the phase each displayed month corresponds to. The POC uses hardcoded session data with no structured timeline, so any month-specific narrative now would be arbitrary tense-shifting rather than real contextual coaching.

## What it needs

- Athlete training timeline (phase start date, race date)
- Week-within-phase calculation for any displayed month
- Narrative content keyed to (phase, week) or generated by the AI coach
- Could be AI-generated once the Knowledge Oracle / session history is richer

## Blocked by

- Structured athlete timeline / Training Plan data model
