# The Idea

Biohacking Coach is an AI coaching product for people training for an Ironman triathlon.

Most training apps hand you a schedule. This one gives you a **Coach**: an AI agent that holds your full context — your race target, your current training phase, how your recent sessions actually went — and runs a weekly ritual *with* you rather than dictating a plan at you.

Each week the Coach reads your check-in and your reflections on the sessions you did, drafts the coming week, and then **negotiates** it with you as a peer backed by training science — not as a form to fill in. Skip a session and nothing breaks: no alarm, no guilt prompt. The context simply gets picked up at the next weekly session. Between sessions you can talk to the Coach and it already knows who you are.

An optional human **Head Coach** can oversee linked athletes, so the AI and a real coach work together rather than compete.

The goal is for it to feel like a coaching relationship you can genuinely delegate to — not a schedule generator with a chat box bolted on.
